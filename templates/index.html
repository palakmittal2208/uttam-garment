Return & Exchange

Our hassle-free policy

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for manufacturing defects or wrong items. The product must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging.

Exchange for size or colour is possible within 7 days. Shipping charges for exchange are borne by the customer unless the fault is ours.

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days via original payment method or as store credit.

Sale items, custom-stitched garments, and items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return or exchange.
